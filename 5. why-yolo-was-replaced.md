# Why YOLO Was Replaced in the 25–100 Camera Architecture

In the **25–100 camera surveillance architecture**, **YOLO is completely replaced** by **RF-DETR** (or **RT-DETRv2**) for object detection and **SCRFD** for face detection. You do not need YOLO anywhere in this pipeline.

## 1. Component-by-Component Replacement Comparison

| Previous Role | New Replacement | Why the Switch Helps at 25–100 Cameras |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Person & Object Detection** (YOLOv12) | **RF-DETR** or **RT-DETRv2** | **No NMS Bottleneck:** YOLO requires Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS) post-processing, which slows down batched multi-camera inference. RF-DETR is end-to-end NMS-free and carries an **Apache 2.0 license** (avoiding Ultralytics AGPL-3.0 commercial/deployment restrictions). |
| **Face Detection** (YOLOv12-Face) | **SCRFD** (`scrfd_10g`) | **Faster & Lighter:** SCRFD is purpose-built for face detection and 5-point facial landmark alignment, running faster and more accurately on cropped person regions than a general-purpose YOLO model. |
| **Tracking** (Ultralytics ByteTrack) | **NVIDIA NvDCF** or standalone **ByteTrack** | **Hardware Offload:** NvDCF runs directly inside DeepStream on GPU/VIC hardware without tying tracking to the Ultralytics Python wrapper. |

## 2. Where Each Model Sits Now

* **Stage 1 (Detection):** **RF-DETR** (`RF-DETR-S` / `RF-DETR-M`) finds every `person` bounding box across your 25–100 camera batches.
* **Stage 2 (Tracking):** **NvDCF / ByteTrack** assigns and maintains each person's `track_id` across frames.
* **Stage 3 (Face Crop & Alignment):** **SCRFD** locates the face and aligns eyes, nose, and mouth inside the person crop.
* **Stage 4 (Recognition):** **ArcFace / AdaFace + FAISS** matches the aligned face against your database to return the person's name.

> **Optional Drop-In Flexibility:** If you already have a custom-trained **YOLOv12 weapon or package detector**, you can still drop it into **Stage 1** in place of RF-DETR, as both take batched frames and output standard `[x1, y1, x2, y2, confidence]` bounding boxes.
